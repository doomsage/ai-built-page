07:00 - morning

MORNING BLUE

The kind of blue that only exists in animation cels and real Augusts. Clouds drift like they have nowhere to be, and the light is still deciding what kind of day this will be.

12:00 - midday

HIGH LIGHT

Cumulus towers stack up over the city. Everything is overexposed, washed in white, the way Shinkai paints noon: too bright to look at, too beautiful to look away.

17:30 - golden hour

GOLDEN HOUR

The sun drops and the whole world turns to honey. Light rays lean through the gaps in the clouds. This is the hour every train window in every film is waiting for.

19:00 - dusk

VIOLET DUSK

Orange gives up to pink, pink gives up to violet. The clouds catch fire one last time. Somewhere below, a door is waiting to be closed.

22:00 - night

FIRST STARS

The sky deepens into indigo and the first stars come out, shy at first, then everywhere. Keep scrolling and the night fills in behind you.
