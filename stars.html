ORION

the hunter

Three belt stars in a row, the easiest hello in the winter sky. In a Shinkai film this is the constellation the characters name when they run out of things to say.

CASSIOPEIA

the queen

A big W tipped on its side, circling the pole all night. She was punished with eternity in the sky, which honestly sounds more like a reward.

LYRA

the lyre

Vega burns blue-white at its corner, the fifth brightest star up there. Summer Triangle anchor, and the star a whole genre of wistful synth tracks is named after.
