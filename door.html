01 / the threshold

There are places
that remember us.

A wooden door in a sea of grass. The last warmth of the sun on its frame. Nothing holds it up, and yet it stands - waiting for someone brave enough to turn the handle.

Open it. Close it. Stay a moment between what is and what might be. The world on the other side is made of starlight.
